# Telehealth Coverage FAQ

_Owner: Member Services - For representative use on calls_

## Is telehealth covered?

Yes. Video and audio-only visits with in-network providers are covered at the same cost share as an in-person office visit. HarborNow virtual urgent care visits have a $0 copay on most commercial plans.

## What place of service code do providers use?

POS 02 for telehealth when the member is not at home and POS 10 when the member is at home, with modifier 95 for synchronous video visits and modifier 93 for audio-only.

## Are out-of-state providers covered?

Only if the provider is licensed in the state where the member is located at the time of the visit.
