# Member Services Call Escalation SOP (MS-ESC-05)

Owner: Member Services · Effective: April 15, 2026

## Tier structure

- **Tier 1 — Member Services Representative (MSR):** eligibility, ID cards, benefits questions, claim status,
  provider search.
- **Tier 2 — Senior MSR / Team Lead:** billing disputes, complex claims, repeat callers, complaints.
- **Tier 3 — Supervisor / Grievance Coordinator:** formal grievances, regulator inquiries, media or legal
  threats.

## Escalate immediately (warm transfer, do not schedule a callback)

1. The member expresses intent to harm themselves or others. Stay on the line, follow the Crisis Protocol
   (MS-CRISIS-01) and connect to the 24/7 Behavioral Health line.
2. The member reports they cannot get urgent medication or care today (for example insulin, an inhaler, or a
   discharge medication). Transfer to the Pharmacy Urgent Access desk or UM Urgent line.
3. The caller identifies as a state regulator, attorney, or member of the media. Transfer to Tier 3.
4. A provider requests an **expedited** prior authorization appeal (see UM-APL-03).

## Escalate to Tier 2 (within the call)

- The member has called 3+ times in 30 days about the same issue.
- A claim adjustment over $1,000 is needed.
- The member disputes a balance bill from an in-network provider.
- 340B-related provider billing questions: transfer to Pharmacy Provider Help Desk queue **PH-340B**
  rather than Tier 2.

## Grievances

If a member says they want to "file a complaint" or are dissatisfied with Harborline, a provider, or a
decision, it is a grievance. Log it in the grievance system during the call. Standard grievances are resolved
within 30 calendar days; expedited grievances (related to an expedited appeal denial) within 72 hours.

## Documentation

Every escalation needs a call note with: reason, what was tried at the current tier, who it was transferred
to, and the reference number given to the member. Never promise a specific outcome.
