# Prior Authorization Appeals: Standard Operating Procedure (UM-APL-03)

Owner: Utilization Management · Effective: March 1, 2026

## Who can appeal

A member, the member's authorized representative, or the treating provider acting on the member's behalf may
appeal a prior authorization (PA) denial. Authorized representatives need a signed Appointment of
Representative form (HH-AOR-1) on file, except for providers requesting an expedited appeal.

## Deadlines

| Step | Standard | Expedited |
|---|---|---|
| Member files appeal | within 60 days of the denial notice | within 60 days |
| Harborline decision (pre-service) | 30 calendar days | 72 hours |
| Harborline decision (post-service) | 60 calendar days | not applicable |

An appeal is **expedited** when the treating provider states that waiting the standard time could seriously
jeopardize the member's life, health or ability to regain maximum function. Customer service must not
second-guess a provider's expedited request; log it and transfer to the UM Appeals line immediately.

## Steps

1. **Intake.** Create an appeal case in the UM system within 1 business day. Record the original PA number,
   the denial reason code and how the appeal arrived (phone, fax, portal, mail).
2. **Acknowledge.** Send the acknowledgment letter (template UM-L-12) within 5 calendar days for standard
   appeals. Expedited appeals are acknowledged by phone.
3. **Clinical review.** A physician reviewer who was **not** involved in the original denial and who has
   expertise in the relevant specialty reviews the case. Peer-to-peer calls with the treating provider are
   offered on every denial upheld for medical necessity.
4. **Decision.** Overturn, partially overturn, or uphold. Upheld decisions must cite the specific clinical
   criteria (for example InterQual or internal medical policy number) used.
5. **Notify.** Mail the decision letter and, for upheld decisions, include external review rights.

## External review

If an appeal is upheld, the member may request an independent external review within 4 months of the final
internal decision. Harborline must send the case file to the independent review organization within 5
business days of the request.

## Common denial reasons and what to ask for

- **Not medically necessary:** request recent clinical notes, imaging and failed prior therapies.
- **Step therapy not completed:** request pharmacy history or chart notes showing the preferred drugs were
  tried and failed or are contraindicated.
- **Out-of-network provider:** ask whether an in-network provider is available within access standards; if
  not, the appeal may be treated as a network adequacy exception.
